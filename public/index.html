Penguin Push

Waddle. Shove. Splash!

How to play

  • 🕹️ Drag your thumb to waddle (or WASD / arrow keys).
  • 👋 Tap Push! (or Space) to slap with your flippers.
  • 🌊 Shove the other penguins into the sea. Last penguin standing wins!
  • ❤️ You have 5 lives. Heart jars give you another.
  • 🐟 Fish jars give powers: ❄️ snowballs (they make penguins dizzy), 🛷 belly slides, 💪 mega flippers.
  • ⛄ Walk into a giant snowball on the hill to send it rolling!
  • 🧊 After 75 seconds the ice starts to crack…
